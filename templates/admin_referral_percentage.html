Important Notes

  • Referral percentage is calculated from approved account value
  • Percentage change only applies to future approvals
  • Existing referral balances won't be recalculated automatically
  • Referrer must exist (i.e., user was referred by someone)
  • You can manually adjust referral balances from User Details page

Referral Strategy Tips

📈 Viral Growth (20%)

High percentage encourages referrals. Fast growth but higher costs.

⚖️ Balanced (10-15%)

Reasonable incentive without breaking the bank. Steady growth.

💰 Cost-Effective (5%)

Minimal referral costs. Focus on direct signups instead.

🎯 Combine with Commission

Use % for existing users + fixed amount for new referrals for maximum incentive.
